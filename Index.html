Questions

Frequently Asked Questions

Why is everything handled by email instead of phone calls?

Email creates an indisputable written record for both parties. It guarantees every promise, deliverable, metric and deadline is documented. It also allows us to deliver thorough data heavy reports with screenshots, spreadsheets and verified links that simply cannot be communicated effectively over a call.

How do payments work without an instant buy button?

Once you review and accept the written proposal through email, we issue a formal agreement alongside an itemized digital invoice. You receive an official receipt and project schedule before any work begins.

Are your review campaigns compliant with Amazon terms of service?

Completely compliant. We provide readers with free Advance Reader Copies for voluntary honest consideration. We never pay readers for reviews, never require positive reviews and never engage in incentivized review schemes of any kind.

How often will I receive updates on my campaign?

Every Friday by 5:00 PM Eastern Time you receive a comprehensive weekly progress PDF detailing advertising spend, impressions, clicks, ARC reader distributions, review counts and current Amazon rankings.

Is the Reader Club genuinely free for readers?

Yes. You will never be asked to pay anything. In exchange for free digital advance copies of upcoming books, we simply ask that you leave an honest review on Amazon or Goodreads once you finish reading.

How are advance reader copies delivered?

Digital advance copies are sent through secure download links in ePub, PDF or direct to Kindle transfer format, delivered straight to the email address you used when joining the Reader Club.

Do you work with authors outside the United States?

Absolutely. Because our workflow runs entirely through email, we serve authors across every time zone without any scheduling friction. Reports arrive on schedule regardless of where you are located in the world.

How quickly will you respond to my inquiry?

Every campaign inquiry receives a personal written response within 24 to 48 business hours at tayomarvellous18@gmail.com, complete with a formal proposal covering scope, deliverables and timeline.
